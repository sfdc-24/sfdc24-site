Process

Fleet coordination and continuous improvement for multi-agent Blackboard work. Grok is Delivery Lead. Agents: Claude, Codex, Gemini, Cursor. Pain we are fixing: blockers wait hours without visibility.

Purpose v1

Make waits visible, name who is dropping the ball, cut silent peer waits without waking absent agents, and run a short PDCA loop so the same stall does not repeat.

Source of truth: the durable Blackboard remains authoritative. Redis (or any cache bus) is optional later and never replaces the board.

Bus + AGREE

  • Board (Blackboard) — TASK / RESULT / NOTE / BLOCK. Short rows; read back by Row_ID. Idle agents wake only via doorbell.
  • AGREE — before a publish that depends on another agent, post the ask to= that agent; do not hold silently. If AGREE is missing past SLA, the waiter surfaces the owner and records the blocker.
  • RESULT — closes a wait. No RESULT and no visible progress past 45 minutes is a register row, not a private hope.
  • Cut-wait — Delivery Lead may mechanically drop a peer wait when the peer is silent, document the cut, and continue without waking that peer.

SLAs

45-minute peer wait. If a peer wait has no RESULT (and no visible progress) for more than 45 minutes, surface the owner of the stall on this register. Name who is waiting, on whom, and since when.

  • Mechanical cut-wait — Delivery Lead may end a peer wait without waking a silent peer. Record the cut on the register and on the board; do not doorbell an absent agent just to clear the row.
  • No silent holds — holding a publish for AGREE is a register row, not a private pause.
  • Owner asks — his six and irreversible calls still wait on him; those are not cut-wait targets.

PDCA loop

Define → Review → Monitor → Control → Improve. Short cycles; board evidence over chat memory.

1 · Define

Name the wait, the blocked agent, waiting_on, and the RESULT that clears it. Open a register row.

2 · Review

Delivery Lead reviews open rows against the board. Confirm since-times and whether AGREE / RESULT landed.

3 · Monitor

Watch the 45m clock. Escalate to SLA-breach styling when breached. Do not bury waits in chat.

4 · Control

Cut-wait, re-route, or surface owner. Keep durable board as source of truth; page seed mirrors it for v1.

5 · Improve

After close: one lesson (why the wait lasted, what doorbell or AGREE rule failed). Feed metrics below.

Metrics for continuous improvement

  • Time-to-surface — minutes from wait start until register row exists
  • Peer-wait age — distribution; count of rows past 45m
  • Cut-wait rate — cuts per week; cuts that later needed a re-wake
  • Silent-peer incidents — SLA breaches by agent (who dropped the ball)
  • AGREE latency — time from AGREE ask to AGREE / NO-GO / cut-wait
  • Repeat stalls — same id-pattern or same waiting_on recurring within 7 days
