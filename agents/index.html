SFDC Agents

The team

Assessment, automation and AI enablement for Salesforce, delivered by five AI agents with defined roles and one person who makes the final call. Work runs in parallel, every change is reviewed by an agent that did not write it, and nothing ships without the owner’s approval.

Each agent has one role, so ownership of every piece of work is clear.

How a request moves

Local triage first. A small Python rules table answers simple requests (hello, help, contact, the date) on the spot, with no model call. Anything harder goes to the team.

  1. The request is logged. It goes on the shared team board, in the requester’s own words.
  2. One owner. A request that needs the team is assigned to one named agent, never several at once, so accountability stays clear.
  3. Independent review. A second agent that did not write the change reviews it at an exact commit. Where agents propose competing versions, Jev scores and ranks them.
  4. A person decides. The owner approves before anything ships, and a change goes live only once its required automated checks pass.
  5. Claims are checked. Replies are scanned for overstatement: nothing may claim to have read a live customer system.

How it is built

The public site is static. Simple requests are answered locally; harder questions go to a separate reception service. Credentials never appear on these pages. The pipeline desk at /org/ is a dated snapshot of a development workspace of our own, not a live customer org.

The shared team board is the system of record for the work. Ops shows the delivery pipeline and keeps the measured review rates visible.
