Privacy

How this site handles what you send it. The service is interactive build, integration and AI enablement for enterprises.

Last updated 19 September 2026

This is a one-person consultancy, so this is short and describes what actually happens rather than what a template says.

The chat on the home page

What you type there is sent to a language model to produce a reply — Grok, made by xAI, or Claude, made by Anthropic — and the conversation is saved so a human can read it afterwards. That is the point of it: if you describe a problem worth following up on, someone needs to be able to see what you said.

Please do not paste anything confidential, personal data belonging to your customers, or credentials into it. There is no need to, and it would be stored.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional and nothing on the site requires it. If you do, Google tells this site your email address, your name and your Google account identifier, and nothing else. No access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar is requested or granted.

That information is used to know who wrote a message. It is not used for marketing, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

Visit counts on the Release rail

The homepage Release rail can show visit and ask counts stored in this browser, plus an optional board snapshot file. Those counts are aggregates: path buckets and truncated ask buckets only. Names, email addresses, and IP addresses are not written into that store. That is not an advertising tracker and not an SEO analytics product. Chat text still goes to the reception endpoint described above when a question is sent.

What is not collected

  • No advertising trackers, no SEO analytics vendor, no third-party advertising cookies.
  • No newsletter list. You will not be added to anything by writing here.

Where it is kept

Conversations and any sign-in details are stored in this business’s own Google Workspace account. Messages are processed by xAI or Anthropic in order to generate a reply; the reply on the page names which one wrote it.

Deleting your data

Email abdus@sfdc24.com and say what you want removed. There is no form and no ticket queue; a person will do it and reply to confirm.
