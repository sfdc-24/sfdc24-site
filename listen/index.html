Streaming speech, captured here

Audio is captured at 16 kHz and sent to a relay over a socket. Words appear while they are still being spoken, and the finished sentence is sent on to the question path this site already uses. No credential for the transcription service reaches this page — that is what the relay is for.

An open microphone is metered, so a spoken conversation has three minutes to reach a problem somebody could be paid to solve. Once it does, the clock stops and the conversation carries on. Typing is never on a timer.
