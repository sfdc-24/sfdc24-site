What this site is

An interactive build, integration and AI enablement service for enterprises, operating from the Toronto area. It exists so you can describe a problem and find out quickly whether it is worth a conversation.

It is useful for working out whether there is something here worth pursuing. It is not professional advice, it can be wrong, and nothing it says creates an engagement or an obligation on either side.

Do not make a change to a production Salesforce org because a chat window suggested it. Paid work is agreed separately, in writing.
