Bring an idea.

A focused place to talk through a business process automation challenge and shape a design. Begin with the outcome you want; the live canvas can take shape as you speak.

What this space is for

Bring a workflow, customer need, or rough concept. Explain who it serves and what would make it useful.

The voice conversation and canvas below share one session. Conference entry is a separate owner flow.

  • 02“Map a team’s work intake process.”
  • 03“Sketch a customer journey for this service.”

02 / Live workspace

Make it visible.

The controls and canvas appear here when the voice service is available. Anything shown by the canvas comes from this conversation.

If voice controls do not appear, the service may be unavailable. You can still use the owner conference entry on Home when that separate flow is open.

This page does not connect authenticated Redis tasks, issues, plans, acknowledgments, or conference context to the voice canvas yet. No activity or completed work is shown until its service provides it.
